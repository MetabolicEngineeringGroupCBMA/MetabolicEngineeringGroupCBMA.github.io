# One-Step Yeast Transformation (Chen et al., 1992)

Rapid one-step transformation of _S. cerevisiae_ (especially stationary phase cells) using LiAc/PEG/DTT. No pre-made competent cells required.

## Materials

- Yeast cells (stationary phase preferred; up to ~2.5 × 10⁸ cells/mL)

- YPD medium

- Plasmid DNA (50 ng – 1 µg)

- Single-stranded carrier DNA (~50 µg)

- **ONE-STEP buffer**:
  - 0.2 M lithium acetate (LiAc)
  - 40% PEG 3350
  - 100 mM DTT
  - pH 5.0

## Protocol

1. Spin down yeast culture (stationary phase).
2. Resuspend to ~5 × 10⁸ cells/mL.
3. Cells (final ~5 × 10⁷ cells per reaction)
4. ONE-STEP buffer
5. Plasmid DNA (50 ng–1 µg)
6. ssDNA carrier (50 µg)
7. Vortex thoroughly.
8. Incubate at **45 °C for 30 min**.
9. Plate directly on selective medium.
10. 30 °C for 3–4 days.

## Key Parameters

- **Temperature:** 45 °C optimal (42 °C or 48 °C reduces efficiency)
- **DTT:** ~100 mM optimal
- **Incubation time:** 30–60 min
- **Cell density:** ~5 × 10⁷ cells per 100 µL
- **Efficiency:** ~10⁴ transformants/µg DNA

## Notes

- Works with:
  - Stationary phase (recommended)
  - Exponential phase cells
  - Fresh colonies or stored cells
- PEG can be increased (e.g., 60%) for _S. pombe_
- Alternative reducing agents (e.g., β-mercaptoethanol, glutathione) may also work
- Suitable for scaling to many samples

# Reference

Chen, D.-C., Yang, B.-C., & Kuo, T.-T. (1992). One-step transformation of yeast in stationary phase. _Current Genetics, 21_(1), 83–84. <https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00318659>
