## Computational Biology vs. Bioinformatics

While often used interchangeably, different emphasis

- **Computational Biology**: Focuses on building theoretical models, running math simulations, and explaining biological mechanisms or evolutionary behavior.
- **Bioinformatics**: Concentrates on organizing, storing, annotating, searching, and managing biological datasets and software pipelines.

## Why learn programming in the age of AI?

Generated code still needs scientific judgment.

- **Specify**: Define the question, inputs, and assumptions
- **Understand**: Read the code; check units and logic
- **Validate**: Test known cases; assess biological meaning

## Code, data, and reproducible research

Could another researcher rerun your analysis?

- **Data**: Original inputs + provenance
- **Code**: Version-controlled instructions
- **Parameters**: Choices + random seeds
- **Environment**: Python + package versions
