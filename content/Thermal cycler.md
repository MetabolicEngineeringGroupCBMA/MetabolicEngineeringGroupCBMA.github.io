# Locking

The locking mechanism of the Biorad T100 thermocycler is made of plastic and breaks easily.

Be careful and open and close slowly.

Do not use excessive force.

Leave the machine open when not used to remove stress on the lock.

![[Thermal.png]]

# Remove "Infinite hold at 12°C"

The final hold step in a thermal cycler is not necessary. The PCR product is perfectly stable for the entire night. In fact, amplified double-stranded DNA is very stable at room temperature for hours to days.

The a long temperature hold at low temperature is bad of the thermal cycler for two reasons:

1. Moisture in the air accumulates inside and will eventually damage the machine
2. Keeping a low temperature stresses the machine’s Peltier elements and cooling fans.
