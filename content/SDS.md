[SDS](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/Sodium-dodecyl-sulfate) or sodium dodecyl sulfate or sodium lauryl sulfate.

To prepare a 20% (w/v) solution, dissolve 50 g of electrophoresis-grade SDS in 200 mL of H2O.
Heat to 68°C and stir with a magnetic stirrer to assist dissolution.
If necessary, adjust the pH to 7.2 by adding a few drops of concentrated HCl.
Adjust the volume to 250 mL with H2O.
Store at room temperature.
Sterilization is not necessary.
Do not autoclave.
