Add this to a tall plastic container:
4 eggs
1 dl olive oil
5-6 "sardeller"
2-3 tbsp dijon mustard
3-5 pressed cloves of garlic
1 tbsp worcesteshire sauce
2 pinches sal
1 pinch black pepper

mix with a mixer until smooth.
add brown sugar to taste

Finally add 1 dl Parmesan cheese

Cut chicken breast into strips or use "stroganoff" chicken.
Grill on high heat with olive or sunflower oil.
Cut the chicken in 1-2 cm pieces.
Season with som combination of:
- smoked paprika
- black pepper
- brown sugar
- sal
- Taco spice mix
- oregano

Mix a salad bag, chicken, croutons and sauce in a large bowl.
