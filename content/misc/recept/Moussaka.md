# Moussaka

![[misc/recept/Moussakaz24g4b.png|373x242]]

Adapted from [Greek moussaka by Tom at Pinch of Mint](https://www.pinchofmint.com/recipes/moussaka). Credit for the original recipe belongs to Tom. This version is scaled from 8 to 12 servings, with whole-vegetable quantities rounded up and the potatoes and aubergines baked instead of fried. The idea for this came from the youtube video comment section below.

[Watch the recipe video on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GOJgZle2oiM)

## Ingredients

### Vegetables

- 5 aubergines
- 5 large potatoes
- Olive oil, for lightly coating the vegetables

### Meat sauce

- 1/2 dL olive oil
- 2 large onions, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1.2 kg beef/lamb mince
- 2 tsp cinnamon
- 2 tsp dried thyme
- 100 g tomato paste
- 5 bay leaves
- 600 g chopped tomatoes
- 300 ml beef stock/red wine
- Salt, pepper

### Béchamel

- 1.5 l whole milk
- 200 g unsalted butter
- 200 g plain flour
- 150 g Parmesan, grated; extra for topping
- 3 egg yolks
- Nutmeg, salt, pepper

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to **200 °C**. Slice potatoes and aubergines 5 mm thick, lightly coat with olive oil and season with salt. Arrange in single layers on baking trays. Bake for **20 minutes**, turning halfway through, until tender.
2. Soften onions in olive oil, 4–5 minutes; add garlic, 2 minutes. Brown mince. Add spices and paste, 1 minute; add remaining sauce ingredients. Simmer 25–30 minutes.
3. Warm milk. Melt butter; stir in flour, 2 minutes. Gradually whisk in milk until thick. Add cheese, nutmeg, yolks and seasoning.
4. Mix 8 tbsp béchamel into meat. Layer potatoes → half the aubergine → meat → remaining aubergine → béchamel → extra Parmesan.
5. Bake at **180 °C fan**, **45–50 minutes**, until golden. Rest **45 minutes** before portioning.

- Remove bay leaves before assembling.
- Take the béchamel off the heat before stirring in the yolks.
- Use two dishes for 6 portions eachif possible.
- Bake the vegetables in batches if needed to avoid overlapping slices.
- Reduce the oven to 180 °C fan before baking the assembled moussaka.
- Check for a golden top and a hot centre.
