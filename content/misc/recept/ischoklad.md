## Swedish Christmas Chocolates

![[ischoklad.png|512x493]]

[source](https://www.thespeltkitchen.com/two-ingredient-swedish-christmas-chocolates-ischoklad/#the)
### Ingredients

- 200  g chocolate You can use absolutely any chocolate you like. This time, I made mine with milk chocolate.
- 100 g coconut fat. In Sweden we have blocks of something called _kokosfett_. The direct translation is "coconut fat". If you can't access it, you could use coconut oil, but the end result might vary a little. See the main post for more information.
- nuts - optional you can top with candy cane, crushed cookies, fruit, or whatever else you fancy!
- flavors - optional Add a drop of mint essence, rum or cognac to the mixture (just stir it into the melted chocolate and oil/butter).
# Steps:

- Gently melt the chocolate and coconut fat in a water bath. Be careful not to over-heat, all you need is for to just melt.
- Pour into small [candy cases](https://www.recepten.se/utrustning/ischokladformar.html) (the above ingredients made 40 small candies), or a candy mould. Sprinkle with whatever you fancy.
- Allow the chocolates to harden (if you're in a hurry, pop them in the fridge for a while). Enjoy!
