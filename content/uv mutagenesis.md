## Survival Curve

1. Culture _Saccharomyces cerevisiae_ yeast cells in YPD medium until the end of the exponential growth phase (~10⁸ cells/mL). After harvesting and washing under aseptic conditions, resuspend the cells in sterile deionized water to the original culture volume. Prepare a dilution to reach an OD of ~0.3 or ~10⁷ cells/mL. Vortex the diluted suspension thoroughly.
    
2. Label 5 sterile tubes: **t0, t15, t30, t60, t90**. Add 1 mL of cell suspension to the first tube (**t0 – non-irradiated cells**).
    
3. Place 1 mL of the cell suspension into an empty sterile Petri dish. Position it at a predetermined distance from a UV lamp. Turn on the lamp for **15 seconds** turn it off and, then transfer the cells into tube **t15**.
    
4. Repeat step 3 using increasing irradiation times: **30, 60, and 90 seconds**.
    
5. Perform serial dilutions as indicated and plate **100 µL** of each dilution onto YPD medium.

1. Incubate plates at 30°C in the dark. After **2–3 days**, count colonies. 
    Construct a **survival curve** on semi-log paper (% viable cells vs. UV exposure time), using **t0 as 100% survival**.
    
## Obtaining and Selecting Mutants

1. Subject cells to UV treatment using a time that yields **5–10% survival**.  
    **Note:** Under typical class conditions, this corresponds to **~30 seconds**.
    
2. After appropriate dilution, plate cells on YPD medium to obtain approximately **500 colonies per plate**.
    
3. After **3 days** incubation at **27°C in the dark**, transfer colonies using **replica plating** onto YP medium supplemented with **glycerol**.
    
4. By comparing plates, identify colonies that **fail to grow on lactic acid medium**.
