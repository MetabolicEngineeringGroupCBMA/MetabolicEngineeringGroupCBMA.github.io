**lacUV5** is a mutant of the _Escherichia coli lac_ promoter used to drive recombinant gene expression.

Two substitutions change its −10 element from `TATGTT` to the σ⁷⁰ consensus `TATAAT`, increasing transcription and reducing dependence on activation by cAMP–CAP (CRP).

With a lac operator and LacI repressor present, expression remains repressible and can be induced with IPTG.
